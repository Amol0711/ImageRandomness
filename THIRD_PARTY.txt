THIRD-PARTY MATERIALS

The fixed_image_and_power/natural_images/inputs directory contains numerical
center crops of the Camera, Clock and Grass grayscale assets distributed with
scikit-image 0.25.2. These fixed reference arrays are computational inputs.
They are the only external image inputs in this archive. Their sources, crop
origins, upstream-byte hashes, array hashes and third-party credits are in
fixed_image_and_power/natural_images/input_manifest.json and THIRD_PARTY.txt.
The two Camera and Grass sources have CC0 status; Clock was released into the
public domain by its photographer according to the upstream documentation.
These source rights are separate from the status of the original software.

Third-party software packages are runtime dependencies and are not vendored.
The requirements file specifies NumPy, SciPy and mpmath. Optional re-fetching
of the upstream PNG inputs additionally uses Pillow 12.3.0. Consult those
packages' upstream distributions for their licenses.
